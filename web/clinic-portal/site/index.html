Clinical Innovation

IM First Framework

Our own Dr. Hadley was among the earliest clinicians in the regenerative medicine space to formalize and systematize an intramuscular-focused biologic administration model, developed with the objective of optimizing clinical response patterns observed in practice.

While many early biologic implementations relied heavily on intravenous infusion models, Dr. Hadley explored alternative administration strategies based on tissue targeting principles, absorption dynamics, and repeatable application within real-world clinical settings. Over time, this IM-centered framework demonstrated consistent treatment response patterns in his practice.

Clinics implementing this model often find that the structured IM approach not only supports therapeutic consistency but also integrates smoothly into existing practice workflows.

Partner clinics receive access to these proprietary implementation frameworks and treatment structures developed through years of clinical refinement.
