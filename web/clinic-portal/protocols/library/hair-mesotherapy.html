Hair & mesotherapy
NOVA-ELITE 2 cc meso gun · optional NOVA-FLEX 1–2 cc deltoid IM
Mesotherapy delivery places MSC-derived exosomes and signaling factors densely into dermal and superficial tissue layers to support localized regeneration, collagen remodeling, and tissue repair. Optional intramuscular NOVA-FLEX adds systemic MSC signaling.

Preparation
  • •Confirm no active infection at the treatment site
  • •Avoid NSAIDs 24–48 h prior if possible
  • •Review contraindications (pregnancy, malignancy, etc.)
  • •Obtain informed consent
  • •Seated or supine by treatment area; expose and sterilize the region
  • •Draw 2 cc NOVA-ELITE into the cartridge; clear air bubbles
Administration
MESOTHERAPY
  1. Set meso gun depth by indication.
  2. Deliver microinjections in a grid-like pattern, 0.5–1 cm apart.
  3. 0.01–0.05 cc per injection; 2 cc total evenly distributed.
DELTOID IM (OPTIONAL)
  1. Inject 1–2 cc NOVA-FLEX into the deltoid muscle.
  2. 25–27 G needle; single or split injection sites.
POST-PROCEDURE INSTRUCTIONS
  • •Avoid strenuous activity 24–48 h
  • •Avoid NSAIDs 48 h
  • •Hydrate well
  • •Expect mild redness or swelling
SAFETY
  • •Observe 5–10 minutes post-procedure
  • •Monitor for infection or adverse reaction; maintain sterile technique
SCHEDULE
Initial
Every 2–4 weeks × 3 sessions
Maintenance
Every 3–6 months
EXPECTED RESPONSES
  • •Improved tissue quality and regeneration
  • •Enhanced collagen and elastin production
  • •Reduced inflammation and pain in MSK application
  • •Visible change typically within 1–4 weeks

WHAT TO EXPECT
Changes are gradual — do not judge the result in the first days
FIRST 24–48 HOURS
Pinpoint redness, tenderness, and tiny bumps at the injection points are normal, as are small spots of dried blood. These settle within a day or two.
WEEKS 1–4
A temporary increase in shedding can occur and is part of the normal hair cycle. No visible change is expected yet.
MONTHS 2–4
The earliest changes in density and texture appear here. Results are judged across the full series using your photos.
DO
•Sleep with your head slightly elevated tonight if tender
•Wash gently after 24 hours with a mild shampoo
•Take acetaminophen (Tylenol) for tenderness if needed
•Keep every session in the series — spacing matters
AVOID
•Washing or wetting the scalp for 24 hours
•Hair coloring or chemical treatments for 1 week
•Tight hats or helmets for 24 hours
•Heavy sweating exercise for 24 hours, and scratching the sites
CALL US RIGHT AWAY IF
For an emergency, call 911 first
•Spreading redness, warmth, or swelling of the scalp
•Pustules, crusting, or drainage at injection points
•Fever above 101 °F
•A severe or unusual headache
