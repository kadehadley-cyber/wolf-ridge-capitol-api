On receipt
  • •Inspect the shipper for damage before opening; photograph anything compromised.
  • •Read and retain the temperature indicator or data logger.
  • •Confirm every lot and quantity against the packing list and the accompanying COA.
  • •Transfer to the correct storage unit immediately — do not stage at room temperature.
  • •Record receipt on the chain-of-custody log (page 18) and file the COA.
Ongoing monitoring
  • •Dedicated biologics unit — no food, specimens, or shared clinic stock.
  • •Continuous electronic monitoring with audible and remote alarm.
  • •Manual temperature check twice daily, initialled on the unit log.
  • •Documented backup plan: emergency power or a validated transfer unit.
  • •Annual calibration of thermometers and loggers, records retained.
EXCURSION
If a unit alarms or a shipper arrives warm: quarantine the affected lots, label them DO NOT USE, record the duration and highest temperature reached, and contact CelluNOVA before any clinical use. Product exposed to an undocumented excursion should not be administered.
