Intranasal neural protocol
NOVA-ELITE 2 cc via intranasal catheter · NOVA-MSC FLEX 1–2 cc deltoid IM
Intranasal administration through the olfactory and trigeminal pathways bypasses the blood–brain barrier. Exosomes may support reduction of neuroinflammation, neuronal repair signaling, and modulation.

Preparation
  • •Confirm no active sinus infection, severe congestion, or recent nasal surgery
  • •No nose blowing for 30 minutes before the procedure
  • •Review contraindications (pregnancy, active infection, recent chemotherapy)
  • •Obtain informed consent
  • •Seated in a treatment chair, head tilted back 30–45° to expose the olfactory cleft
  • •Draw 2 cc into sterile syringes (1 cc per nostril); remove air bubbles
  • •Attach syringes to the catheter per manufacturer instructions
Administration sequence
1
Left nostril. Insert catheter into position; instill 1 cc slowly over 10–20 s. Maintain head-back position and hold 8–10 minutes. Instruct the patient not to sniff heavily or swallow forcefully.
2
10-minute interval. Keep the head back; wait 10 minutes before the second nostril.
3
Right nostril. Instill 1 cc slowly over 10–20 s; maintain head-back position another 8–10 minutes.
4
Deltoid IM. Administer 1–2 cc NOVA-MSC FLEX intramuscularly.
POST-PROCEDURE INSTRUCTIONS
  • •No nose blowing for at least 60 minutes
  • •Avoid bending forward for 20–30 minutes
  • •Hydrate normally; avoid alcohol the same day
  • •Mild throat drainage sensation is normal
SAFETY
  • •Observe 5–10 minutes post-procedure
  • •Monitor for sinus discomfort or infection
  • •Consider ENT clearance for severe deviated septum

EXPECTED RESPONSES
  • •Reduced hyperarousal, intrusive thoughts, and nightmares
  • •Improved emotional regulation and cognitive clarity
  • •Typical onset 3–7 days; fuller change at 8–12 weeks

WHAT TO EXPECT
Changes are gradual — do not judge the result in the first days
FIRST FEW HOURS
A drip at the back of the throat, an odd taste, mild congestion, or sneezing are all normal, along with soreness at the arm injection site.
FIRST WEEK
Nothing dramatic is expected. Keep your symptom diary daily — small notes beat big impressions.
WEEKS 4–8
We review your diary against baseline at follow-up and decide whether a repeat treatment makes sense.
DO
•Stay reclined at the clinic for the full observation period
•Keep the daily symptom diary we gave you
•Drink water normally through the day
•Take acetaminophen (Tylenol) for arm soreness if needed
AVOID
•Blowing your nose forcefully for 4–6 hours
•Nasal sprays for 24 hours unless prescribed
•Alcohol for 72 hours
•Anti-inflammatory pain relievers for 72 hours
CALL US RIGHT AWAY IF
For an emergency, call 911 first
•A nosebleed that does not stop with gentle pressure
•A severe headache or a stiff neck with fever
•Fever above 101 °F
•New confusion, or trouble breathing
