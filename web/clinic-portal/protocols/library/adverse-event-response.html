REFERENCE · SAFETY
Adverse-event response
Recognize, treat, document, report
TIER 1 · MILD
Expected, self-limited
Injection-site soreness, bruising, transient swelling or flare, mild headache after intranasal delivery.
Reassure, ice, acetaminophen preferred over NSAIDs, document in the record, follow up at 72 hours.
TIER 2 · MODERATE
Needs assessment
Fever, escalating pain past 72 h, spreading erythema or warmth, effusion, persistent rash or urticaria.
Examine same day, consider aspiration and culture, imaging, antibiotics or antihistamine as indicated, notify the ordering physician.
TIER 3 · SEVERE
Emergency
Anaphylaxis, airway or breathing compromise, hypotension, syncope, septic joint, neurologic deficit.
Stop immediately, run the sequence at right, activate EMS, transfer to emergency care, notify CelluNOVA within 24 hours.
Suspected anaphylaxis — immediate sequence
1Stop administration. Call for help and start the code timer.
2Epinephrine IM into the mid-outer thigh — 0.3–0.5 mg adult, 0.15 mg pediatric per weight. Repeat every 5–15 min as needed.
3Activate EMS. Position supine with legs elevated unless breathing is compromised.
4High-flow oxygen, IV access, isotonic fluid bolus for hypotension.
5Adjuncts only after epinephrine: antihistamine, inhaled bronchodilator, corticosteroid.
6Monitor continuously and transfer — biphasic reactions can occur hours later.
Follow your clinic's current emergency protocol and ACLS/PALS training; the above is a reminder, not a substitute for it.
Documentation & reporting
  • •Record onset time, symptoms, vitals, treatment given, and outcome in the chart the same day.
  • •Capture product, lot, expiry, thaw time, volume, site, and guidance used.
  • •Quarantine any remaining product from the same lot and retain packaging.
  • •Notify CelluNOVA within 24 hours for any Tier 2 or Tier 3 event: 801.787.2123.
  • •Report serious adverse events to FDA MedWatch and to your state authority as required.
  • •Review the event at the next quality meeting and record any protocol change made.

DISCHARGE INSTRUCTIONS TO THE PATIENT
Expect soreness for 24–72 hours. Call the clinic for fever, spreading redness, escalating pain, or drainage; call 911 for breathing difficulty, throat tightness, faintness, or a spreading rash. Give the patient a printed copy with a 24-hour contact number.
