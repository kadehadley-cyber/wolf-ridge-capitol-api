CelluNOVA
SYSTEMIC · IM SUPPORT
Spina bifida support
Regenerative biologic adjunct · intramuscular administration
An adjunctive framework intended to support neural signaling, reduce neuroinflammation, and support systemic repair mechanisms alongside standard medical and surgical management. This therapy is not intended to cure spina bifida and is investigational and off-label.
Inclusion
  • •Diagnosed spina bifida (myelomeningocele, meningocele, or occulta)
  • •Pediatric or adult patients per clinic policy
  • •Medically stable without active infection
  • •Informed consent obtained
Exclusion
  • •Active systemic infection
  • •Active malignancy
  • •Pregnancy unless approved by the Medical Director
Dosing & administration
REGIMEN A · TOTAL
5 cc
EXM 2 cc + PV-MAX 3 cc, IM
NEEDLE / OBSERVATION
22–25 G
observe 15–30 min post-procedure
REGIMEN B · NOVA PRODUCT DISTRIBUTION (7 cc TOTAL)
  • •NOVA-MSC FLEX: 2 cc per glute, bilaterally
  • •NOVA-CORE: 1.5 cc per glute, bilaterally
  • •Preferred site: gluteal musculature (volume tolerance, systemic absorption)
  • •Alternative: deltoid, maximum 2 cc per deltoid
  • •Aspirate prior to injection and inject slowly
  • •Regimens A and B are alternatives, not additive — confirm which applies before dosing.
PREPARATION & HANDLING
  • •Thaw according to manufacturer specifications
  • •Glycerol-based products do not require washing
  • •Use within 15–30 minutes post-thaw
  • •Maintain strict sterile technique throughout
TREATMENT SCHEDULE
Single initial administration. Repeat dosing may be considered every 4–8 weeks based on clinical response; re-evaluate after 2–3 sessions.
ADJUNCTIVE CARE
Continue standard medical and surgical management. Consider nutritional optimization (B-complex, magnesium, omega-3) and physical or neuro-rehabilitation as indicated.
OUTCOME MEASURES
NEUROLOGIC STABILITY PAIN / SPASTICITY FUNCTIONAL MOBILITY QUALITY OF LIFE
RISKS & SAFETY
Injection-site discomfort, bruising, or transient inflammatory response; potential site infection. No known tumorigenic risk has been identified based on the acellular nature of the biologics.
OVERSIGHT
All treatments must be ordered and supervised by a licensed physician experienced in regenerative medicine, and must comply with applicable state and federal regulations, institutional policy, and informed-consent requirements. No claims of disease cure are made.
Investigational and off-label use. Not FDA-approved to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease.

WHAT TO EXPECT
Changes are gradual — do not judge the result in the first days
FIRST 48 HOURS
Soreness at the injection sites is common, and a low-grade temperature for under 24 hours can occur. Normal activity as tolerated is fine.
FIRST WEEKS
Physical therapy, bracing, and bladder and bowel routines continue exactly as before — this treatment adds to that care, never replaces it.
BY FOLLOW-UP
Bring your notes and diary. Progress is measured against PT goals and the baseline exam, not day-to-day impressions.
DO
•Keep every physical therapy appointment
•Bathe normally after 24 hours
•Use acetaminophen (Tylenol), dosed by weight, for soreness
•Keep the bladder and bowel routine unchanged
AVOID
•Anti-inflammatory pain relievers for 72 hours unless your physician says otherwise
•Hot soaking baths for 24 hours
•Massage of the injection sites for 48 hours
•Judging results in the first days
CALL US RIGHT AWAY IF
For an emergency, call 911 first
•Fever above 101 °F
•Spreading redness, warmth, or swelling at an injection site
•Unusual sleepiness or lethargy
•New weakness, or any change in bladder or bowel function
