A systemic wellness and longevity approach: NOVA-FLOW given as a slow IV push into a running 250 mL saline drip, followed by a spaced sequence of supportive micronutrients — with at least one minute between each push to monitor tolerance.
Indications
  • •General wellness and vitality optimization
  • •Healthy-aging and longevity support
  • •Antioxidant and mitochondrial support
  • •Energy, recovery, and immune support
  • •Adjunct to a physician-directed wellness program
Contraindications & cautions
  • •Active infection or sepsis
  • •Known hypersensitivity to any component (cobalamin, thiamine, NAC, zinc)
  • •Uncontrolled coagulopathy
  • •Fluid-overload states (decompensated CHF, significant renal impairment) — caution with the 250 mL bolus
  • •Pregnancy or breastfeeding unless cleared by the treating physician
Procedure
PREPARATION
  1. Review history, medications, and allergies; obtain informed consent.
  2. Establish patent IV access (20–22 G).
  3. Hang 250 mL 0.9% saline; start a slow-to-moderate drip and confirm patency, no infiltration.
  4. Verify each additive, dose, and product integrity before use.
  5. Have anaphylaxis / emergency supplies on hand.
IV PUSH SEQUENCE — ≥ 1 MIN BETWEEN EACH
  1. NOVA-FLOW 2–4 cc — slow IV push into the running line.
  2. Vitamin B-12 (methylcobalamin) — 500 mcg.
  3. Thiamine (B1) — 100 mg.
  4. NAC (N-acetylcysteine) — 600 mg.
  5. Zinc — 4 mg.
Push each slowly and pause ≥ 1 minute between pushes, watching for flushing, chest tightness, taste change, or hypotension. Then let the remaining saline infuse to completion.
POST — PATIENT INSTRUCTIONS
  • •Hydrate well over the next 24 h
  • •Report any delayed rash, wheezing, or swelling
  • •Resume normal activity as tolerated
  • •Space treatments per the physician plan
FOLLOW-UP & OUTCOME MEASURES
Reassess energy, recovery, and wellness goals at 2–4 weeks. Repeat on a physician-directed cadence (e.g., every 2–4 weeks) based on response and tolerance.
