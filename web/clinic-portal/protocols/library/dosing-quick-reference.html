READ FIRST
This page is a summary only and is not a substitute for the full protocol. Spina bifida regimens A and B are alternatives, not additive. Confirm every value against the protocol page and the lot-specific COA before drawing a dose.
VOLUME LIMITS BY IM SITE
  • •Gluteal: up to 3 cc per side in adults — preferred for larger volumes
  • •Deltoid: maximum 2 cc per side
  • •Vastus lateralis: pediatric and small-frame patients per weight
  • •Split any dose exceeding the site limit across sites, not into one site
  • •Aspirate before injecting and inject slowly at every IM site
  • •Observe 15–30 minutes after any systemic or intranasal administration
BEFORE EVERY DOSE — FIVE CHECKS
PATIENT ID PRODUCT & LOT EXPIRY VOLUME & ROUTE CONSENT ON FILE
Read the five checks aloud with a second staff member present and initial the chain-of-custody log before injection.
IF ANYTHING DOES NOT MATCH
Stop, return the vial to storage, and resolve the discrepancy with the ordering physician before proceeding. A mismatch between order, label, and COA is never corrected at the bedside.
