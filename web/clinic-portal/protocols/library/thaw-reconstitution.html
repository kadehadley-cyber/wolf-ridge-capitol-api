Thaw sequence — cryopreserved product
1Confirm patient, indication, product, lot, and expiry against the order and the COA.
5Inspect for particulates, discoloration, or a compromised seal — do not use if present.
2Prepare the field, syringes, needles, and guidance equipment before removing product.
6Draw the full dose using aseptic technique; expel air without aerosolizing.
3Thaw in a 37 °C dry block or water bath, keeping the cap above the water line.
7Note the thaw time on the record — administration is timed from this point.
4Remove as soon as the last ice crystal clears; swirl gently, never vortex or shake.
8Administer within the working window; discard any unused volume per policy.

Do not
  • •Vortex, shake, or sonicate — mechanical stress degrades the product.
  • •Warm above 37 °C or thaw in a microwave.
  • •Refreeze, pool lots, or hold a drawn syringe for a later patient.
  • •Filter through a 0.22 µm filter unless the label directs it.
  • •Co-mix with anesthetic, corticosteroid, or contrast in the same syringe unless directed.
  • •Use product with a missing or mismatched COA.
