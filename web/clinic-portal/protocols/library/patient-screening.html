CelluNOVA
REFERENCE · SCREENING
Patient screening
Applies to every indication, in addition to protocol-specific criteria
Universal inclusion
  • •Diagnosis documented and consistent with the selected protocol
  • •Medically stable, no active systemic or local infection
  • •Realistic expectations discussed, including that outcomes vary and are not guaranteed
  • •Able to attend follow-up and complete outcome measures
  • •Written informed consent obtained before the procedure (page 16)
Absolute exclusion
  • •Active systemic infection, sepsis, or infection at the intended site
  • •Active or untreated malignancy
  • •Pregnancy or breastfeeding, unless approved by the Medical Director
  • •Known hypersensitivity to a product component or diluent
  • •Inability to give or have a proxy give informed consent
RELATIVE — DOCUMENT THE DECISION
Uncontrolled diabetes, active autoimmune flare, therapeutic anticoagulation, immunosuppression, recent corticosteroid injection at the site, or recent live vaccination.
Pre-procedure checklist
History, exam, and imaging reviewed for this indication
Anticoagulant or antiplatelet hold discussed with prescriber
Infection screen: no fever, no active skin lesion at site
NSAIDs and corticosteroids addressed per protocol
Pregnancy status confirmed where applicable
Allergy history reviewed, including anesthetic and diluent
Baseline outcome measures recorded (pain, function, photos)
Emergency medications and anaphylaxis kit checked in date
Consent signed, witnessed, and filed
Product, lot, expiry, and COA verified against the order
Post-procedure instructions printed and reviewed with patient
Transport home arranged where sedation or IN delivery is used
Guidance equipment available and tested where the protocol calls for it
Second staff member available for the five pre-dose checks (page 14)
Chain-of-custody line opened for each vial (page 18)
Follow-up appointment booked before the patient leaves
Medication guidance
NSAIDS
Consider holding 5–7 days before and 2 weeks after, using acetaminophen for comfort where appropriate.
CORTICOSTEROIDS
Avoid injection at the same site within 4–6 weeks before or after, unless clinically necessary.
ANTICOAGULATION
Any hold is decided with the prescribing physician; never stop therapy on the day of the procedure alone.
