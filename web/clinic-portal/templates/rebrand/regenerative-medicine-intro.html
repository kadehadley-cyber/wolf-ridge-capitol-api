An Introduction to Regenerative Medicine

Our practice offers regenerative therapies — treatments that use biologic products such as mesenchymal stromal cells (MSCs) and exosomes to support the body's own repair processes. This sheet answers the questions patients ask most often.

What are these therapies?

Regenerative biologics are derived from carefully screened, laboratory-tested donor tissue. They contain cells and cell-derived signaling factors that researchers believe can help modulate inflammation and support tissue repair. Your provider will explain which product, dose, and delivery method fits your situation.

What conditions are they used for?

  • Joint discomfort and orthopedic conditions (knee, hip, shoulder, spine)
  • Soft-tissue and sports injuries
  • Selected inflammatory and autoimmune-associated conditions
  • General wellness and recovery support

What should I expect?

Most treatments are completed in a single office visit, delivered by injection or IV infusion. Your provider will review preparation, aftercare, and follow-up with you, and every treatment starts with a screening visit to confirm you're a good candidate.

Is this right for me?

That's a conversation with your provider. Bring your health history and current medication list to your consultation, and ask every question you have — informed patients get the best outcomes.

These therapies are not FDA-approved to treat, cure, or prevent any disease, and individual results vary. This material is educational and is not a substitute for a medical consultation. Ask your provider for the full informed-consent discussion before any treatment.
